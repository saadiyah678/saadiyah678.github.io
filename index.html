About

Dynamic and research-driven chemist with a Master of Science in Chemistry from the University of Sharjah, where I graduated with a perfect CGPA of 4.00/4.00 and ranked first in my cohort in the College of Sciences. I also hold a Bachelor of Science (Hons.) in Chemistry with Highest Honors from the University of Sharjah.

I am currently a PhD student in Chemistry at Khalifa University of Science and Technology and a Full-time Research Assistant Scholarship Award recipient. My research focuses on electrochemical resource recovery and density functional theory (DFT) investigations, with particular interests in electrocatalysis, reaction mechanisms, and computational materials design for sustainable energy applications.

Previously, I served as a Graduate Research Assistant at the University of Sharjah, where I conducted research in seawater electrolysis, anti-corrosion strategies and advanced functional materials. I also completed my term as President of the American Chemical Society International Student Chapter at the University of Sharjah, leading scientific initiatives, outreach activities, and professional development programs that fostered collaboration and student engagement.

Passionate about scientific research, innovation, and interdisciplinary problem-solving, I am committed to advancing sustainable technologies through the integration of experimental and computational chemistry.
